Replication package

FORMA

Architectural inputs enter one classifier. Formation is a separate policy. A tactic is an explicit copy, reassessed and compared with the baseline.

A · Architectural inputs

1 · Verification context CDelegate to provider. Designated verifier v.
2 · Constraint φPredicate, required attributes, accepted strengths.
3 · Evidence configuration EVisibility, form, and issuer.

Available evidence reaches the verifier. The oracle is not an input.

B · Verification and formation

1 · Validate policyOwnership and evidence form.
2 · Evidence usabilityPermitted witness and accepted strength.
3 · Constraint evaluationholds, violated, or unavailable.
4 · CheckabilityFeasibility and the attributes read.
Formation policyCheckable and holds leads to Accept. Otherwise Block. Escalation preconditions lead to Defer.

C · Tactic assessment

1 · Check applicabilityAn explicit caller invokes the tactic.
2 · Transform a copySelective disclosure, trusted attestation, or public certification.
3 · Reassess alternativeThe same classifier and formation policy as B.
4 · Compare resultsCheckability, evaluation, formation, disclosure, and issuer reliance.

The constraint formula is retained.
